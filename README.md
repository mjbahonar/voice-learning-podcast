# Learn Norwegian: Listen & Repeat

A podcast of listen-and-repeat Norwegian lessons for English speakers. Each episode explains a pattern, drills phrases at normal and slow speed, leaves silent gaps for you to speak, and ends with a dialogue and role play. The voices are computer generated.

This repository is only the hosting for the podcast. GitHub Pages serves the RSS feed and the audio files from it.

- **Podcast feed:** https://mjbahonar.github.io/voice-learning-podcast/feed.xml (paste it into any podcast app: Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Pocket Casts, Castbox, Overcast, AntennaPod)
- **Web page:** https://mjbahonar.github.io/voice-learning-podcast/

Based on the free online course *Norsk på nett* (NOW) from NTNU, the Norwegian University of Science and Technology. The audio and exercises are my own study material. This podcast is not affiliated with or endorsed by NTNU.
